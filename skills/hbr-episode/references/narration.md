# Writing for the ear and marking the delivery

Every script goes to the owner in two layers: the words (`scenes.json` "ar", what the captions show) and a
performance copy (`"perf"`, `script_for_recording.txt`) with delivery marks.

## Writing for the ear
- Put the listener in the scene: «تخيّل إنك واقف..», «انتبه للكلمة..». Talk to one person.
- Short beats. One fact per sentence; break long sentences at the turn: «وراه زملاءه.. حوالي أربعين طلقة.»
- Repeat the key word as its own beat: «كم سنتيمتر.. بين عالمين.» / «شجرة.. كادت تشعل حرب.»
- End each part on a turn or a question, so the next part is a payoff.
- Name the feeling with one concrete image, not adjectives («المدن اللي صارت رماد», «أكله ذرة، وبطنه مليان ديدان»).
- Facts stay exactly as researched; drama comes from rhythm and order, never from invented details.

## Marks
`/` short pause · `//` long pause · `..` stretch, don't cut · `**word**` stress · `[أبطأ] [أسرع] [هادي] [همس] [دافي]` tone.

## Notes that go on top of every recording sheet
- Tell it like a story to a friend at night, not like news; a slight smile in the voice keeps the listener close.
- Names and numbers slower than the rest. Questions rise, then half a second of silence.
- Heavy moments: lower and slower, no acting; let the silence work. Action: faster, shorter.
- A mistake: stop two seconds and repeat the whole sentence; the alignment removes the bad take.
- The channel intro is pre-recorded and inserted after the hook automatically: mark its place in the script.
