# Retention review (a week after publishing)

Ask the owner for screenshots from YouTube Studio → the video → Analytics:
1. **Audience retention** graph (Engagement tab) with the "typical retention" comparison.
2. **Key moments**: intro retention (first 30 s), top spikes, top dips.
3. **Reach**: impressions, click-through rate (CTR), traffic sources.
4. For shorts: "viewed vs swiped away", and how many went on to the long video.

Read them like this:
- First 30 s below ~65 %: the hook is weak or slow — next time open on motion and the question sooner.
- A sharp dip: map its time to the scene (`<slug>_times.json`) and see what happens there (long setup, music
  change, repeated idea, intro too long). A spike: something people re-watch — do more of that.
- Steady slope down with no dips: pacing is fine; the topic decides the rest.
- CTR under ~4 %: thumbnail/title problem, not the video. Test a second thumbnail (YouTube "Test & compare").
- Write 3 concrete changes for the next episode into `lessons.md`.
